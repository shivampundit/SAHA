Part 1

Measures of Central Tendency & Dispersion (TPSC PYQ Focus)

11 One-Liners
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] The Mean of a dataset is the arithmetic average of all values: μ = (Σx_i) / n.
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] Mean is the measure of central tendency affected the MOST by extreme values (outliers); Median is robust against outliers.
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] Variance (σ²) is the square of the Standard Deviation (σ), equal to the average of squared differences from the Mean: σ² = E[(X - μ)²] = E[X²] - (E[X])².
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] Coefficient of Variation (CV) is a dimensionless relative dispersion metric calculated as: CV = (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100%.
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] Linear distribution is NOT a valid standard probability distribution (Normal, Poisson, Exponential, Binomial, Uniform are valid).
  • ⚡Empirical Relationship (Karl Pearson's formula for moderately skewed distributions): Mode = 3 × Median - 2 × Mean.
  • ⚡For a symmetric distribution (such as Normal distribution): Mean = Median = Mode (Skewness = 0).
  • ⚡For a positively (right) skewed distribution: Mean > Median > Mode; for a negatively (left) skewed distribution: Mean < Median < Mode.
  • ⚡Effect of Change of Origin and Scale: If Y = aX + b, then Mean E[Y] = aE[X] + b, Variance Var(Y) = a²Var(X), and Standard Deviation σ_Y = |a|σ_X (adding a constant b does NOT change Variance or SD!).
  • ⚡Mean Deviation is minimum when measured around the Median; Sum of squared deviations Σ(x_i - c)² is minimum when c = Mean.
  • ⚡Harmonic Mean (HM), Geometric Mean (GM), and Arithmetic Mean (AM) for positive numbers satisfy: AM ≥ GM ≥ HM, and GM² = AM × HM (for two numbers).
Part 2

Probability Axioms, Conditional Probability & Bayes' Theorem

9 One-Liners
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] Probability of rolling an even number {2, 4, 6} on a fair 6-sided die is 3/6 = 1/2.
  • ⚡[TPSC PYQ 2025] If two events A and B are statistically independent, then P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B), and P(A | B) = P(A).
  • ⚡Addition Theorem of Probability (Inclusion-Exclusion): P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B).
  • ⚡Mutually Exclusive (Disjoint) Events: A ∩ B = ∅, so P(A ∩ B) = 0 and P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B).
  • ⚡Important Trap: Two events with positive probabilities P(A)>0, P(B)>0 CANNOT be both mutually exclusive and independent simultaneously!
  • ⚡De Morgan's Laws in Probability: P(A' ∩ B') = P((A ∪ B)') = 1 - P(A ∪ B); P(A' ∪ B') = 1 - P(A ∩ B).
  • ⚡Conditional Probability Formula: P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B), valid whenever P(B) > 0.
  • ⚡Law of Total Probability: If B_1, B_2, ..., B_n partition the sample space, P(A) = Σ P(B_i) P(A | B_i).
  • ⚡Bayes' Theorem (Posterior Probability): P(B_k | A) = [P(B_k) P(A | B_k)] / [Σ P(B_i) P(A | B_i)].
Part 3

Random Variables, Expectation, Covariance & Correlation

7 One-Liners
  • ⚡For any valid discrete PMF p(x): p(x) ≥ 0 and Σ p(x) = 1; for any continuous PDF f(x): f(x) ≥ 0 and ∫_{-∞}^{∞} f(x) dx = 1.
  • ⚡For any continuous random variable X, the probability at a single exact point is always zero: P(X = c) = 0, so P(a ≤ X ≤ b) = P(a < X < b).
  • ⚡Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF): F(x) = P(X ≤ x); PDF is the derivative of CDF: f(x) = dF(x)/dx, and P(a < X ≤ b) = F(b) - F(a).
  • ⚡Linearity of Expectation: E[aX + bY] = aE[X] + bE[Y] holds ALWAYS for all random variables, even if X and Y are dependent!
  • ⚡Variance of Sum/Difference: Var(aX ± bY) = a²Var(X) + b²Var(Y) ± 2ab Cov(X,Y); if X and Y are independent, Cov(X,Y) = 0 so Var(X ± Y) = Var(X) + Var(Y).
  • ⚡Covariance Formula: Cov(X,Y) = E[XY] - E[X]E[Y]; if X and Y are independent, Cov(X,Y) = 0 (converse is NOT always true).
  • ⚡Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r): r = Cov(X,Y) / (σ_X σ_Y), which always lies in the range -1 ≤ r ≤ +1.
Part 4

Standard Probability Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential, Normal)

9 One-Liners
  • ⚡Binomial Distribution B(n, p): PMF P(X = k) = C(n,k) p^k q^(n-k); Mean = np, Variance = npq (where q = 1 - p). In Binomial distribution, Mean > Variance always (since 0 < q < 1).
  • ⚡Poisson Distribution Pois(λ): PMF P(X = k) = (e^(-λ) λ^k) / k!; Mean = λ and Variance = λ (Mean equals Variance is the signature property of Poisson!).
  • ⚡Poisson Approximation to Binomial: Used when n → ∞ (large trials) and p → 0 (rare event) such that λ = np stays constant.
  • ⚡Continuous Uniform Distribution U(a, b): PDF f(x) = 1 / (b - a) on [a, b]; Mean = (a + b) / 2, Variance = (b - a)² / 12.
  • ⚡Exponential Distribution Exp(λ): PDF f(x) = λe^(-λx) for x ≥ 0; Mean = 1/λ, Variance = 1/λ², and P(X > t) = e^(-λt).
  • ⚡Memoryless Property: Exponential is the ONLY continuous memoryless distribution (P(X > s+t | X > s) = P(X > t)), and Geometric is the ONLY discrete memoryless distribution.
  • ⚡If inter-arrival times between packets follow Exponential(λ), the number of arrivals in time T follows Poisson(λT).
  • ⚡Normal (Gaussian) Distribution N(μ, σ²): Bell-shaped, symmetric around μ; Standard Normal Z = (X - μ)/σ has Mean = 0 and Variance = 1.
  • ⚡68–95–99.7 Rule for Normal Distribution: 68.27% within μ ± 1σ, 95.45% within μ ± 2σ, and 99.73% within μ ± 3σ; Kurtosis of Normal distribution (β_2) = 3 (Excess Kurtosis = 0).
