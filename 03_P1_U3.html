Part 1

Tripura History, Statehood & Royal Manikya Dynasty

11 One-Liners
  • ⚡Rajmala is the royal court chronicle of the Manikya dynasty of Tripura, originally composed in Bengali verse under Maharaja Dharma Manikya I (15th century).
  • ⚡Tripura Merger Agreement was signed by Maharani Kanchan Prava Devi (Regent for minor Kirit Bikram Kishore Manikya) on 9 September 1949.
  • ⚡Tripura officially merged with the Indian Union as a Part-C State on 15 October 1949.
  • ⚡Tripura became a Union Territory on 1 November 1956 (under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956).
  • ⚡Tripura attained Full Statehood on 21 January 1972 under the North-Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act, 1971 (along with Manipur and Meghalaya).
  • ⚡Maharaja Bir Bikram Kishore Manikya Bahadur (1908–1947) is revered as the 'Father of Modern Architecture in Tripura' (planned Agartala & founded MBB College in 1947).
  • ⚡Ujjayanta Palace (Agartala) was built by Maharaja Radha Kishore Manikya between 1899 and 1901 (designed by Sir Alexander Martin of Martin & Burn Co.).
  • ⚡Neermahal ('Water Palace' in the middle of Rudrasagar Lake at Melaghar, Sepahijala) was built by Maharaja Bir Bikram Kishore Manikya in 1930.
  • ⚡Bhubaneswari Temple (Udaipur, on the bank of River Gomati) was built by Maharaja Govinda Manikya (immortalized in Rabindranath Tagore's novel 'Rajarshi' and play 'Bisarjan').
  • ⚡Tripura Sundari Temple (Matabari, Udaipur — one of the 51 Shakti Peethas, shaped like a tortoise 'Kurma Pitha') was built by Maharaja Dhanya Manikya in 1501 A.D.
  • ⚡First Chief Minister of Tripura: Sachindra Lal Singh (1963–1971); First Governor of Tripura: B. K. Nehru (1972).
Part 2

Tripura Geography, Polity, TTAADC & Culture

17 One-Liners
  • ⚡Total geographical area of Tripura is 10,491.69 sq. km (3rd smallest state in India after Goa and Sikkim).
  • ⚡Tripura is surrounded by Bangladesh on three sides (North, West, and South) sharing an 856 km international border (84% of its total perimeter).
  • ⚡Tripura shares domestic state borders with Assam (53 km in the northeast) and Mizoram (109 km in the east).
  • ⚡The Tropic of Cancer (23.5° N latitude) passes directly through Udaipur (Gomati district) in Tripura.
  • ⚡Tripura has 8 Districts (created after adding 4 new districts in January 2012) and 23 Sub-Divisions; Dhalai is the largest district by area, and Unakoti is the smallest.
  • ⚡Six Principal Hill Ranges of Tripura (West to East): Baramura, Atharamura, Longtharai, Shakhan, Jampui, and Deotamura.
  • ⚡Highest Peak in Tripura: Betalongchhip (also called Betlingchhip / Thaidawr, 939 metres) located in the Jampui Hills (North Tripura).
  • ⚡Longest River in Tripura: River Manu (167 km, originates in Shakhan Range); Largest river by water volume/discharge: River Gomati (133 km, originates at Dumbur).
  • ⚡River Haora (Saidra) flows past the state capital Agartala.
  • ⚡Dumbur Hydroelectric Project is located on the Gomati River; Monarchak Thermal Power Plant (Sonamura) and Rokhia/Baramura are natural gas power stations.
  • ⚡State Symbols of Tripura: State Animal = Phayre's Langur; State Bird = Green Imperial Pigeon; State Tree = Agar; State Flower = Nageshwar; State Fruit = Queen Pineapple (GI tagged).
  • ⚡Tripura Legislative Assembly has 60 seats (20 reserved for ST, 10 for SC); Parliamentary representation: 2 Lok Sabha seats (Tripura West & Tripura East-ST) and 1 Rajya Sabha seat.
  • ⚡TTAADC (Tripura Tribal Areas Autonomous District Council, HQ at Khumulwng) has 30 seats (28 elected + 2 nominated by Governor); brought under the 6th Schedule via the 49th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1984 (effective 1 April 1985).
  • ⚡Tripura recognizes 19 Scheduled Tribes (Tripuri/Debbarma largest, Reang/Bru second largest, Jamatia, Chakma, Halam, Mog, Garo, Lushai, etc.).
  • ⚡Kokborok Day is celebrated every year on 19 January (Kokborok was recognized as an official state language on 19 January 1979).
  • ⚡Hojagiri is the famous balance folk dance of the Reang (Bru) tribe performed during Mailuma/Garia festival; Garia Puja, Kharchi Puja (14 deities / Chaturdasha Devata), and Ker Puja are major festivals.
  • ⚡Unakoti (famous for 7th–9th century rock-cut Shaivite reliefs, 'One less than a Crore') and Pilak (Buddhist/Hindu terracotta sculptures in South Tripura) are premier heritage sites; Rudrasagar Lake is Tripura's Ramsar Wetland Site (designated 2005).

Part 4

Indian History, Geography, Polity & Science GK

9 One-Liners
  • ⚡Indus Valley Civilization: Harappa (Dayaram Sahni, 1921 on Ravi), Mohenjodaro (R.D. Banerji, 1922 on Indus), Lothal (dockyard in Gujarat), Dholavira (3-part city & water management in Gujarat).
  • ⚡Buddhism & Jainism: Gautam Buddha attained enlightenment at Bodh Gaya and gave his first sermon at Sarnath; 4th Buddhist Council was held in Kashmir under Kanishka.
  • ⚡Mauryan & Gupta Empires: Ashoka's Kalinga War (261 BCE, Rock Edict XIII); Samudragupta is called the 'Napoleon of India'; Aryabhata & Kalidasa flourished in the Gupta era.
  • ⚡Modern Indian History: Battle of Plassey (1757), Revolt of 1857, Indian National Congress founded in 1885 (A.O. Hume, 1st President W.C. Bonnerjee), Partition of Bengal (1905, Lord Curzon).
  • ⚡Gandhian Era: Champaran Satyagraha (1917), Non-Cooperation Movement (1920), Dandi Salt March / Civil Disobedience (1930), Quit India Movement (8 August 1942), Cabinet Mission (1946).
  • ⚡Indian Constitution: Adopted on 26 November 1949, enforced on 26 January 1950; Fundamental Rights (Part III, Art. 12–35), DPSP (Part IV, Art. 36–51), Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art. 51A, 42nd Amendment 1976).
  • ⚡Key Constitutional Amendments: 42nd (Mini-Constitution, 1976), 44th (Right to Property moved to Art. 300A, 1978), 73rd (Panchayati Raj, 1992), 74th (Municipalities), 86th (Right to Education Art. 21A), 101st (GST).
  • ⚡Indian Geography: Standard Meridian of India is 82°30' E (passes through Mirzapur, UP; IST = GMT + 5:30); Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram).
  • ⚡Longest Peninsular River: Godavari ('Dakshin Ganga'); West-flowing rivers into Arabian Sea through rift valleys: Narmada and Tapi; Majuli is formed by the Brahmaputra.
