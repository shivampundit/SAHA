General Mental Ability, Logical Reasoning & Quantitative Aptitude (15 Marks · 15 MCQs) — Direct Exam One-Liners

Part 1

Number Series, Letter Series & Coding-Decoding

6 One-Liners
  • ⚡Prime Numbers from 1 to 100: There are exactly 25 prime numbers (1 to 50 has 15 primes; 51 to 100 has 10 primes; 2 is the only even prime).
  • ⚡Common Series Patterns: Arithmetic Progression (constant 1st difference), Geometric Progression (constant ratio), Two-tier difference, and n² ± 1 or n³ ± 1.
  • ⚡EJOTY Rule for Alphabet Positions: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25 (or CFILORUX for multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24).
  • ⚡Opposite / Complementary Letter Pair Rule: Sum of forward and backward alphabet positions is always 27 (e.g., A=1 & Z=26 -> 1+26=27; M=13 & N=14 -> 27).
  • ⚡Caesar / Shift Cipher Coding: Every letter shifts by +k or -k positions modulo 26.
  • ⚡Matrix / Chinese Coding: Compare two or more coded statements to isolate common words and their corresponding code tokens.
Part 2

Blood Relations, Direction Sense & Ranking

9 One-Liners
  • ⚡Blood Relation Anchor: Always resolve from the possessive pronoun ('my', 'his', 'her') outward step by step.
  • ⚡'Only son of my mother's father' = Maternal Uncle (if speaker is female or has no brother) or Mother's brother.
  • ⚡'A is the brother of B' defines A's gender (Male), but B's gender remains unknown (Male or Female) unless specified.
  • ⚡Direction Sense Pythagoras Rule: Shortest straight-line displacement d = √(Δx² + Δy²); common Pythagorean triplets are (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (6,8,10), (8,15,17), (7,24,25).
  • ⚡Shadow Rule at Sunrise (Morning): Sun is in the East -> all shadows fall toward the WEST (if facing North, shadow falls to your Left; if facing South, to your Right).
  • ⚡Shadow Rule at Sunset (Evening): Sun is in the West -> all shadows fall toward the EAST (if facing North, shadow falls to your Right).
  • ⚡Turn Arithmetic: 4 consecutive Right turns (4 × 90° = 360°) return to the same direction; 1 Right (90° CW) + 1 Left (90° CCW) cancel each other out.
  • ⚡Linear Ranking Formula: Total persons N = (Position from Left + Position from Right) - 1.
  • ⚡Circular Arrangement: Number of circular permutations of n distinct people = (n - 1)!; facing center: Left = Clockwise, Right = Counter-Clockwise.
Part 3

Syllogisms, Logical Deductions & Clocks/Calendars

12 One-Liners
  • ⚡All A are B -> 'Some A are B' and 'Some B are A' are definitely true, but 'All B are A' is possible, NOT definite.
  • ⚡No A is B -> 'No B is A' and 'Some A are not B' and 'Some B are not A' are all definitely true.
  • ⚡Some A are B + All B are C -> Definite conclusion: 'Some A are C' (and 'Some C are A').
  • ⚡Two Particular Premises ('Some A are B' + 'Some B are C') yield NO definite universal or particular conclusion between A and C.
  • ⚡Complementary Pair ('Either-Or' Case): Occurs when two conclusions (e.g., 'Some A are C' and 'No A is C') share the exact same subject-predicate and neither is individually definite.
  • ⚡Clock Angle Formula: Angle between Hour and Minute hands at H hours M minutes is θ = |30H - 5.5M| degrees (if θ > 180°, reflex angle = 360° - θ).
  • ⚡Clock Hands Coincidence (0°): Hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours (22 times in 24 hours) at every 65(5/11) minutes.
  • ⚡Clock Hands Opposite (180°): Hands are in a straight line opposite each other 11 times in 12 hours (22 times in 24 hours).
  • ⚡Clock Hands Right Angle (90°): Hands are perpendicular 22 times in 12 hours (44 times in 24 hours).
  • ⚡Calendar Odd Days: Ordinary year (365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day) has 1 odd day; Leap year (366 days) has 2 odd days.
  • ⚡Leap Year Century Rule: A century year (ending in 00) is a leap year ONLY if divisible by 400 (1600 and 2000 are leap years; 1700, 1800, 1900 are NOT).
  • ⚡Odd Days in Centuries: 100 years = 5 odd days; 200 years = 3 odd days; 300 years = 1 odd day; 400 years = 0 odd days.
Part 4

Quantitative Aptitude (Percentages, Profit-Loss, SI/CI, Work, Speed)

12 One-Liners
  • ⚡Successive Percentage Change (a% and b%): Net % change = a + b + (a × b)/100.
  • ⚡If A is R% more than B, then B is less than A by [R / (100 + R)] × 100%; if A is R% less than B, B is more than A by [R / (100 - R)] × 100%.
  • ⚡Profit % = (SP - CP)/CP × 100 (always on Cost Price); Discount % = (MP - SP)/MP × 100 (always on Marked Price).
  • ⚡Relation between MP and CP: MP / CP = (100 + Profit%) / (100 - Discount%).
  • ⚡Simple Interest: SI = (P × R × T) / 100; Compound Interest: A = P(1 + R/100)^T.
  • ⚡2-Year CI minus SI Shortcut: (CI - SI) for 2 years = P × (R / 100)².
  • ⚡3-Year CI minus SI Shortcut: (CI - SI) for 3 years = P × (R / 100)² × (3 + R/100).
  • ⚡Time & Work Combined Rate: If A takes x days and B takes y days, together they take (x × y) / (x + y) days.
  • ⚡Man-Days-Hours Formula: (M1 × D1 × H1) / W1 = (M2 × D2 × H2) / W2.
  • ⚡Speed Conversion: 1 km/hr = 5/18 m/s; 1 m/s = 18/5 km/hr (e.g., 72 km/hr = 20 m/s; 90 km/hr = 25 m/s).
  • ⚡Average Speed for Equal Distances at speeds u and v: Harmonic Mean = 2uv / (u + v).
  • ⚡Relative Speed: Opposite directions = (u + v); Same direction = |u - v|; Boat Downstream = u + v, Upstream = u - v.
